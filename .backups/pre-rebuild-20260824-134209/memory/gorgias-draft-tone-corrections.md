Corrections Atira made to my drafts on 2026-08-15:

1. **Don't presume anything about what the customer thinks, expects, or was doing.** I wrote "if you've been waiting for a click there, that's why it isn't happening" when the customer had never said where she expected the click. Only reflect back what they actually wrote.
2. **Don't sound like a know-it-all.** Cut framings like "here's the part that trips a lot of people up" and "a lot of people get confused by this." They talk down to the customer.
3. **Explain parts with an everyday comparison**, not just a technical name. "The same kind of spring button that holds a collapsable tent pole together" beat naming it a spring-loaded detent pin.
4. **Soften how a problem is described.** "Slipped down inside the tube" over "fell inside."

**Why:** Atira is resetting Strolee's support voice to be kind and human, and these patterns read as condescending or presumptuous — the opposite of the intent.

**How to apply:** Before handing over a draft, reread it for any sentence that narrates the customer's experience back to them or implies they made a common mistake. Cut it and state the fix plainly. See [[gorgias-approved-reply-example]].
