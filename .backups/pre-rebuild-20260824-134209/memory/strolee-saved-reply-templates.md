## Double vs Double Lite

> The Strolee Double Lite is a limited edition stroller which we are offering to combat the rising costs parents are seeing now a days. Its very similar to the current v2 double with a few differences all to be able to keep the costs down considerably.
> The Lite does not come with the accessories like the v2 double does (Backpack, Rain Cover, Snack Trays) these are all available for purchase separately. It also does NOT have the adjust handle bar & deeper seats like the v2. The safety harness system is the 5 point magnetic buckle vs the v2 which has a 3 point with a no rethread harness. On the Lite the parent pockets on the back of the canopies close with velcro where the v2 features zippers.
> The Lite is able to hold 50 LBS per seat & the v2 is 60 LBS per seat. Both are tested down to 3 months of age
> The fabrics, wheels, belly bar, seat cushion, canopy size are all the same & all the accessories are interchangeable between to 2 items.
