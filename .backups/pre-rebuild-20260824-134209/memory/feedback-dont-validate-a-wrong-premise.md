Don't open with "you're right to keep pushing on this" or any variant when the customer's premise is wrong. State the fact kindly and go straight to what happens next.

**Why:** Atira, 2026-08-22: *"She's not right to keep pushing this."* The customer had bought Baby-stroller wheels from the Baby site and insisted they were advertised for her cart. I validated her first and corrected her second, which makes the correction read as a contradiction and invites another round.

**How to apply:** This is the same instinct as the Playbook's "diagnose bluntly in your head, never in the draft." Neutral fact, then the remedy. Warmth goes in the remedy, not in agreeing with something untrue. See [[gorgias-draft-tone-corrections]], [[strolee-wheel-positions]].
