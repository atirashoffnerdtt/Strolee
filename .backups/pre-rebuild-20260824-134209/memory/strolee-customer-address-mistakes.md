When a customer enters the wrong shipping address at checkout, that is the customer's mistake. Strolee will very rarely (almost never) reship or replace in these cases, especially for high-ticket items.

If the customer claims they updated their address but the order still shipped to the old one, the address on the order at checkout is what matters. The carrier delivered where they were told to deliver.

**How to handle:** Be warm but direct. Clarify the facts (correct carrier, what address was on the order). Do not leave the door open for a resolution from Strolee's side. Use "Take care" closing. See [[strolee-fedex-delivery]], [[strolee-ups-delivery]].
