Don't recite order contents, line-item math, discount breakdowns, shipping addresses, or order totals back to a customer. They placed the order. Answer only what they asked, then stop.

**Why:** On 2026-08-22 I drafted a reply to a customer who asked for a "$30 off code" she had already used automatically. My draft itemized her whole order to prove I'd checked. Atira: *"Vickis message was all kinds of fucked up."* Her actual sent reply was three sentences: "I see you were able to order with the code, yay! We will ship your order Monday." Showing the research reads like an invoice, not like a person. The research is for me, not for the customer.

**How to apply:** After drafting, delete every sentence that only exists to demonstrate I looked something up. Also note the tense: "we hope you love your Strolee" before it ships, "loving" only once they have it. See [[feedback-minimize-tickets]], [[feedback-loving-your-strolee-closing]].
