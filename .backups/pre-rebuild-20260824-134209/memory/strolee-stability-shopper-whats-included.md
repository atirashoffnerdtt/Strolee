## Included with the Stability Shopper V2

- The personal shopping bag (large bottom bag/basket, 21"L x 12.5"W x 15"D)
- The cart frame itself
- Cup holder

## Separate Add-On Accessories (NOT included)

From the product page (stroleecarts.com/products/strolee-stability-shopper-special):
- Mesh Caddy Bag — $17
- Pet Bassinet v3 — $75
- V2 Stair Climbing Wheels — $42
- Lower Pet Seating — $42
- Personal Shopper Rain Cover — $39
- V2 Beach Wheels — $42
- Deluxe Organizer & Crossbody Bag — $45
- Personal Shopping Bag Replacement — $39
- Strolee Travel Backpack — $39

**Important:** The main product images show the cart with the mesh caddy bag attached, which makes customers think it's included. It is not. The "Personal Shopping Bag Replacement" being labeled as a replacement confirms the shopping bag IS included with the cart.

## Stability Shopper "Special"

The "Special" (e.g., August Special) is just the regular Stability Shopper V2 at a limited-time discount ($165, normally $195). Same product, same inclusions.
- **Special link:** https://stroleecarts.com/products/strolee-stability-shopper-special
- **Regular link:** https://stroleecarts.com/products/strolee-stability-shopper
- Always use the Special link when the sale is active.
- The $40 V1 upgrade discount does NOT stack with the Special price.

**Confirmed:** 2026-08-17 by checking the product page directly.
