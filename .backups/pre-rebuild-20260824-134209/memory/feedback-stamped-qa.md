Stamped.io Q&A tickets (customer questions on product pages) need to be answered, not closed or dismissed as system notifications.

**Why:** These are real customer questions posted on the product page. Answering them helps the asking customer AND future shoppers who see the Q&A.

**How to apply:** When a Stamped.io ticket comes in with a customer question, draft an answer the same way you would any other customer inquiry. Research the answer from product pages and memory if needed.
