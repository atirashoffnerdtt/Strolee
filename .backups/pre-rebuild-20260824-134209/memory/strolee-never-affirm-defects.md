Strolee replies must not concede fault of any kind. This covers two things:

**1. Product problems.** Never affirm or validate a defect, even when the customer describes the failure in detail.

**2. Service failures.** Never apologize for slow replies, missed follow-ups, or a customer having to chase us — even when it is plainly true. Corrected 2026-08-16 on the Sadie Cramer ticket, where I wrote "I'm so sorry you've had to chase this for a month" and "I should have had this resolved for you long before now." Both had to come out.

Reassurance comes from **acting**, not from conceding. "Thank you for following up with me on this. I've taken care of it for you." Then state what was done. Pointing at a third party's failure (a carrier misdelivery) is fine — that is not our fault to admit.

Things I wrote that had to come out (2026-08-16, Kathlene Alvarado, pet stroller front wheels):
- "I'm so sorry about the front wheels, and I really don't like hearing that the dogs have been thrown off." — apologizes for the product and acknowledges a safety incident.
- "They handle uneven sidewalks and curbs much better." — implies the standard wheels are inadequate.

**How to say it instead:**
- Answer the actual question, warmly. "Yes! We do have upgraded front wheels available."
- Frame alternate parts as an **upgrade customers choose**, never as a fix for a shortcoming: "a lot of our customers love them for uneven sidewalks, curbs and everyday use."
- Put the warmth in enthusiasm and in offering help ("just let me know if you have any trouble swapping them on"), not in sympathy for a product failure.
- Sympathy for the *person* is fine. Concessions about the *product* are not.

**Why:** Written admissions of a defect create liability, especially where injury or animals are involved. Atira's call, stated 2026-08-16.

**How to apply:** Before handing over any draft responding to a complaint, reread it for sentences that accept fault, name a defect, or compare a current part unfavorably to a replacement. Rewrite them as neutral or as upgrades. This does NOT override genuine service recovery Atira explicitly authorizes (warranty replacements, refunds) — she can offer those, I just don't editorialize about why. See [[gorgias-reply-tone]].
