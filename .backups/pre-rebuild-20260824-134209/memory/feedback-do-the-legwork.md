When drafting a reply and you're missing information (what a product includes, what a bundle contains, troubleshooting steps), go find the answer yourself. Check the product page on the website, search Gorgias macros for the steps, look at order data. Do not ask Atira to fill in gaps you could have researched.

**Why:** Atira corrected this on 2026-08-17. The whole point of using me for support is to reduce her workload. If I flag every knowledge gap back to her instead of looking it up, I'm just creating more work. She pointed out that I have the same problem in the reels repo — feedback memories exist but I don't consistently apply them before outputting.

**How to apply:** Before drafting any reply, ask: "Do I know enough to answer this?" If not, use the tools available (browser for product pages, Gorgias macros for troubleshooting, Shopify data for orders) to fill the gap. Only flag to Atira when it genuinely requires her judgment or a business decision she hasn't made before.
