When a customer reports a package delivered to the wrong address or not received but marked delivered:

- **Strolee cannot reship** due to the high cost of items
- Customer must **open a case directly with FedEx** to investigate
- They can open a case through the tracking number
- If a delivery photo shows it at an office or somewhere recognizable, mention what the image looks like
- Don't use "Talk soon" on these — use the "Take care" closing since we don't want them reaching back out about this
