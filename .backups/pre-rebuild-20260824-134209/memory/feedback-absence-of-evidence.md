A page fetch returns text, not embedded video, scripts, or anything rendered client-side. "I didn't find it" is not "it isn't there." Don't turn a tool's blind spot into a factual claim to a customer.

**Why:** 2026-08-22, I fetched the stair-climbing wheel product page, got only the text description, and concluded there was no wheel-swap video anywhere on the site. I told two customers that and wrote it into the Playbook. Atira found the video sitting on that exact listing, below the add to cart button, and sent it.

**How to apply:** Product listings carry videos below the add to cart button, not just the how-to pages. Before saying we don't have something, check where it would actually live. If it still can't be confirmed, ask Atira rather than telling the customer no. See [[feedback-do-the-legwork]], [[strolee-wheel-positions]].
