If Strolee is refunding a purchase, the item comes back, no matter how small or how much return shipping costs. Never draft "keep it and we'll refund you anyway."

**Why:** Settled by Atira 2026-08-22: *"We definitely need all items returned. lol"* I had drafted a $45 crossbody-bag refund with "please keep the bag" because return shipping would eat most of the value, citing keep-it examples from the old ticket archive. Those archive one-offs are not the policy. Refunds and returns are one transaction.

**How to apply:** The carve-out is warranty defect replacements and missing-item reships, which are replacements rather than refunds and require nothing back. Everything on the refund side comes home. See [[strolee-return-policy]], [[feedback-pick-one-and-hold-it]].
