NEVER skip the assist line before a "Take care" closing. It must always be there:

> If there's ever anything we can assist you with in the future please don't hesitate to reach out!

Or when the customer owns a Strolee (see [[feedback-loving-your-strolee-closing]]):

> We hope you're loving your Strolee and if we can ever be of assistance in the future please don't hesitate to reach out!

**Why:** Atira corrected this on 2026-08-17 after multiple drafts omitted it. She uses this line on every single "Take care" reply. It's non-negotiable.

**How to apply:** Every draft that ends with "Take care, Atira S, The Strolee Team" MUST have one of these lines directly above it. No exceptions.
