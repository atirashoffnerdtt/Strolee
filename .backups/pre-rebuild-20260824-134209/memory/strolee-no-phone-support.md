Strolee does not have a phone support line. The Contact Us page (stroleecarts.com/pages/contact) lists only:
- Email: connect@stroleecarts.com
- A web contact form
- Physical address: 480 Interchange Dr, Dallas, NC 28034

When a customer asks for a phone number, don't just say "we don't have one." Redirect warmly: "We don't have a phone line, but I'm right here and happy to help you work through it!"
