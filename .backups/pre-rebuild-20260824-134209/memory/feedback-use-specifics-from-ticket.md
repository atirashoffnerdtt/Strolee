When specific details are right there in the ticket, always include them in the draft. Never write a generic version of something when the specifics are available.

**Why:** Atira had to repeatedly correct drafts that said vague things like "your unique affiliate link" when the actual link, code, and commission rate were sitting right in the ticket. It's obvious to include them, and leaving them out creates extra back-and-forth for no reason.

**How to apply:** Before finalizing any draft, re-read the ticket and ask: "Is there anything specific in here that I'm summarizing generically?" If the ticket contains a link, code, order number, tracking number, product name, date, dollar amount, or any other concrete detail that the customer needs, put the actual value in the draft. See [[feedback-minimize-tickets]], [[feedback-do-the-legwork]].
