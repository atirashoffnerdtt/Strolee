Open the Playbook section for the ticket type immediately before drafting. Not once at session start. The file is long and memory of it decays across a queue, and what decays first is the specific step that would have closed the ticket.

**Why:** Atira, 2026-08-22: *"Why didn't you add it if its in the playbook? I dont want to have to correct you over and over again."* I left the refund amount out of a return reply and flagged it to her instead. The rule was in the returns section, which I'd read hours and forty tickets earlier. At least three corrections that day were things already written in the files.

**How to apply:** Also treat "Needs Atira" as a last resort, not a safe default. Before flagging, check whether the Playbook answers it. Flagging documented policy hands her back the work the file exists to save. See [[feedback-follow-skill-steps]], [[feedback-do-the-legwork]], [[feedback-read-the-whole-thread]].
