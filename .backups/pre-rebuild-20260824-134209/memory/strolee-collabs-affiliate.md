## Shopify Collabs Approved Affiliates

When someone is approved through Shopify Collabs and asks what to do or which product to feature:

**Key points to cover:**
1. Welcome to the Strolee family
2. They're approved and set to start sharing
3. **Include their specific details from the Collabs approval email in the ticket:** their affiliate link, their discount code (and what it gives their audience), and their commission rate. Always pull these from the ticket and list them out so the customer has everything in one place.
4. They can share their link and code on social media, blog posts, stories, wherever works best
5. No specific product requirement, highlight whichever Strolee stroller speaks to them and their audience

**Do not** write a generic "you have a unique affiliate link" without including the actual link, code, and commission. The whole point is to give them the details so they can start right away.

**Important:** This is different from unsolicited influencer/partnership PITCHES (those get the "we don't currently have an ambassador program" response per [[strolee-saved-reply-templates]]). Collabs affiliates are already approved through Shopify and get a real welcome.

**Exception:** Atira may direct specific affiliates toward a particular product (e.g., the Double) if that's what the brand needs content for at the moment. In that case she'll override the "no specific product requirement" line.

Use "Talk soon" closing on first reply. See [[gorgias-reply-tone]].
