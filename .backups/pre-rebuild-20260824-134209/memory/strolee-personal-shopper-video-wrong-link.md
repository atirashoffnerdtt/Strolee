Send `stroleecarts.com/pages/stability-shopper-how-to` to a Stability Shopper owner. Never `stroleecarts.com/pages/personal-shopper-assembly`.

**Why:** Corrected by Atira 2026-08-22: *"you sent the incorrect how to. we do not sell the personal shoppers anymore. that's not what she ordered."* The Playbook's folding section listed the personal-shopper-assembly link as the assembly video, so I sent it to a customer whose order was a Stability Shopper V2. The Personal Shopper is a discontinued product. Sending its video tells someone who just bought a current cart to follow instructions for a cart that isn't theirs.

**How to apply:** Check the actual order line before pasting any how-to link. The personal-shopper-assembly page is only for someone who genuinely owns a Personal Shopper. See [[strolee-folding-troubleshooting]], [[feedback-use-specifics-from-ticket]].
