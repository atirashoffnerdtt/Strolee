Asking a customer to go look for something is a favor, not an instruction. "Can you please just confirm it is not in the lower basket for me?" not "have a look down in the lower basket." Normalize the miss by pointing at shipping or at the object, never at people.

**Why:** On 2026-08-22 Atira used macro 262668 over my draft for a missing cup holder: *"I used the macro for becky it sounded more pleasant than the one you gave."* Mine was efficient and correct but it instructed her and then immediately offered the replacement, which made the ask sound like a formality. The macro asks a favor, says "many times in shipping the cup holder ends in the lower basket & blends right in so people don't notice it at first :)", and waits for her answer before offering anything.

**How to apply:** "Many times in shipping it ends up X and blends right in" is warm because the shipping did it. "A lot of people miss it" is the banned know-it-all framing because the person did it. Same fact, opposite feeling. This is a register lesson from one macro, not a licence to copy macro voice generally, [[strolee-past-tone-rejected]] still stands.
