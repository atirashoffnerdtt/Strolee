Every customer-facing reply in Strolee's Gorgias uses a warm, kind tone.

**Opening.** On the *first* reply in a thread, open with:

```
Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out, and I'll be happy to assist!
```

When continuing a conversation already underway, **skip it**. Repeating it mid-thread reads as canned. Go straight into the substance after "Hi [Name]!"

**Closing.** Pick the closing line and sign-off based on whether we need something back:

- **Awaiting a reply** (they owe us an answer, a decision, info):
  ```
  Talk soon,
  Atira
  ```

- **Conversation is finished**, nothing outstanding:
  ```
  If there's ever anything we can assist you with in the future please don't hesitate to reach out!

  Take care,
  Atira S
  The Strolee Team
  ```

**Why:** Atira set the wording 2026-08-15 and refined the opener and sign-off rules 2026-08-16. The sign-off split signals to the customer whether the ball is in their court.

**How to apply:** Applies to every Gorgias reply, macro, and AI-agent draft written for Strolee. Customer-facing messages only — internal notes aren't used at all. Keep the body kind and human in between. See [[gorgias-approved-reply-example]], [[gorgias-sounds-like-ai]], and [[gorgias-draft-tone-corrections]].
