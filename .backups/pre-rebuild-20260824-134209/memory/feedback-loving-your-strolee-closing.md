When the customer already has a Strolee product, use this closing instead of the standard one:

> We hope you're loving your Strolee and if we can ever be of assistance in the future please don't hesitate to reach out!

**Why:** Atira provided this on 2026-08-17. It's warmer and more personal than the generic "If there's ever anything we can assist you with" closing because it acknowledges they're part of the Strolee family.

**How to apply:** Use this closing on any "Take care" reply where the customer is a confirmed owner. The standard closing is for situations where ownership isn't established. See [[gorgias-reply-tone]].
