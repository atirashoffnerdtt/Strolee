Always aim to minimize the number of back-and-forth tickets. Keep replies focused and don't introduce options or information that the customer can't act on or that will generate follow-up questions.

**Why:** Atira wants as few tickets to deal with as possible. Mentioning products or accessories a customer can't use (like V2-only accessories to a V1 customer) just creates confusion and extra replies.

**How to apply:** Before including any product mention or option in a draft, ask: "Can this customer actually use this?" If not, leave it out. Keep the reply tight, actionable, and headed toward resolution. See [[feedback-do-the-legwork]].
