**Three tags mean skip the ticket: `Ask Shad`, `Call Shad`, `Notify when back`.**
Don't draft them, don't research them, don't recommend closing them. List them by number and
subject so Atira can see what's parked, then move on.

- `Ask Shad` / `Call Shad` = parked waiting on Shad. Atira has a shared Gorgias view built on
  these, so they are a live working queue.
- `Notify when back` = the customer is owed a restock notification. Skipping is the handling.

**Why:** Atira, 2026-08-22: *"You need to be skipping tickets with these tags."* She said it three
times in one session before it landed.

**The trap that caused it, and it was mine.** Her first wording was *"ignore the tag 'Notify when
back'."* I wrote that into this file as "ignore it entirely, it says nothing about what the ticket
needs," which reads as "evaluate the ticket normally and draft it." That inference then went into
PLAYBOOK.md, was loaded every session, and produced two replies for Michelle Beach and Dolores
Marquez that should never have been written. **"Ignore the tag" meant skip the ticket.**

**How to apply:** skip on sight, list it, move on. Separately, "no new customer message" is still
not "finished" — a ticket where Strolee owes the next move stays open regardless of tags. And when
Atira's wording is short enough to read two ways, write down her words, not my reading of them.
