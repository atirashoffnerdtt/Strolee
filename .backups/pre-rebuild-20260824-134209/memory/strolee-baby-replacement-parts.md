## Single Belly Bar
- Price: $17.90 (Belly Bar Only) or with Snack Tray option
- URL: stroleebaby.com/products/single-belly-bar-for-strolee-double
- Same belly bar fits ALL Single Strolee strollers
- The Double comes with a 2-section belly bar included. This single bar lets you use one side with car seat adapters.
- Customers may call it a "lap bar"

## Warranty note
Warranty covers manufacturing defects only. Customer-caused damage (chewing, drops, misuse) is not covered. Strolee will very rarely cover a customer's mistake. See [[strolee-customer-address-mistakes]] for similar policy on shipping errors.
