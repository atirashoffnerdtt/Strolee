## Destination Stroller vs Linq
- Both fit in overhead bins
- **Destination:** smaller/more compact, has option for upgraded larger wheels, handle is 40" from floor (2" shorter than Linq), 12.5 lbs, folded: 8.5"D x 17.5"W x 22"H
- **Linq:** can hold a car seat, handle is 42" from the ground, no rear axle (good for taller parents with longer strides)

## Double vs Double Lite
- Double Lite is a limited edition, lower-cost version of the V2 Double
- **Lite does NOT include:** backpack, rain cover, snack trays (available separately)
- **Lite does NOT have:** adjustable handlebar, deeper seats
- **Harness:** Lite has 5-point magnetic buckle; V2 has 3-point with no-rethread harness
- **Parent pockets:** Lite uses velcro; V2 uses zippers
- **Weight limit:** Lite holds 50 lbs per seat; V2 holds 60 lbs per seat
- **Both:** tested down to 3 months of age
- **Same on both:** fabrics, wheels, belly bar, seat cushion, canopy size; all accessories are interchangeable

## V2 Stability Cart Identification
- **Black frame = V2** (compatible with new wheels)
- **Silver frame = V1** (not compatible with new wheels)
- Upgrade offer: $40 off the new V2 cart, customer keeps their current cart

## Discontinued: Linq Bars
- Linq bars to connect strollers are no longer sold, and they will NOT be restocked (confirmed across many 2026 tickets)
- We only sell the strollers themselves now
- The Linq system page is still live and still describes connecting two strollers: https://stroleebaby.com/pages/strolee-linq . Connector bars are not purchasable there, but the page keeps generating "where do I buy the Linq bar" tickets and some public complaints on Facebook about advertising a system that can't be completed
- Reply approach: state plainly that the bars are no longer available and won't be restocked. Do not apologize for the product. Do not promise a future version

## HSA/FSA (Burst)
- Strolee cannot apply HSA/FSA directly or process reimbursement for the customer
- Customer MUST go through Burst themselves
- Website: app.getburst.com
- Email: support@getburst.com
- **Reply approach:** Tell the customer we cannot do this, they have to go through Burst. Point them to the website and email. If they already purchased, an LMN must be dated on or before the purchase date (cannot be backdated per IRS rules), so they may be out of luck unless they already had an LMN.

## Car Seat Adapters (older note, $39, on sale from $55)
- Compatible strollers: Strolee Double V2, Linq V2, Baby+
- Two adapter versions: "Nuna - Maxi Cosi - Cybex - Clek - Britax" and "Chicco - Graco"

  - Bravo Sport = KeyFit Sport (NOT on our list)
  - Bravo LE Trio = KeyFit Max ClearTex (NOT on our list)
  - Bravo Primo Trio = KeyFit Max Zip ClearTex (NOT on our list)
- Our adapter page names ONLY "Chicco Keyfit 30 & 35." **When a customer says "Bravo" or just "KeyFit," ask which KeyFit model they have before confirming compatibility.** "KeyFit" alone is not enough
- **Open question for Atira/Shad:** does the Chicco/Graco adapter fit the KeyFit Max or KeyFit Sport? Not stated anywhere on the site
- Baby+ and Double stroller can hold car seats; mobility Strolee cannot (yet)
- Link: https://stroleebaby.com/products/strolee-double-car-seat-adapter
