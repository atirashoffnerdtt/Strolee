Same policy as FedEx delivery issues (see [[strolee-fedex-delivery]]):
- Strolee cannot reship due to the high cost of items
- Customer must open a delivery claim directly with UPS using their tracking number
- UPS investigates where the package was left and works toward resolution

When a customer says "UPS says it's on you," don't concede. The carrier handles delivery investigation once the package is in their hands.

If the customer's address was wrong on the order (e.g., old address they didn't update), that's the customer's error, not Strolee's or UPS's. Ask to confirm their current address and compare to the order before directing them to UPS.

**Closing:** Same as FedEx — use "Take care" on the final reply (once directing to UPS), not "Talk soon," since we don't want them reaching back out about this. If you're still asking for info (like address confirmation), use "Talk soon" until the final reply.
