Strolee carts and strollers use small spring-loaded metal snap buttons to lock frame parts together. **They sit on the UNDERSIDE.** Always tell the customer to look underneath the bar or arm — this is the single most important line in any of these replies, and I have had to be reminded of it more than once. Press the button in, slide the piece on, and the button pops back out through a hole to lock it. No screws are involved anywhere in this.

Where they show up:
- **Stability Shopper cross bar (manual step 3)** — buttons are on the **underside of the bars on the cart**, not on the cross bar. The two halves of the cross bar slide together and do **not** click into each other; the click comes from the cart's buttons.
- **Wheels into the frame** — described in the Wheel Removal macro as "a silver tab on each side"; press and hold to slide the wheel out.
- **Double v2 rear axle** — "where the axle connects to the frame there is a silver button"; push it and the axle slides off.

**Common failure:** the button slips down inside the tube during shipping, so there's nothing to press. Fix: drop a pen into the top of the tube and give it a gentle push, and the button pops back out.

**How to word the fix.** Two things Atira corrected me on 2026-08-16:
- Say the button **pops OUT**, never "pops up." The button is on the underside, so "up" sends the customer looking at the top of the bar.
- Don't tell them to line the button up with the hole themselves. The sequence is: press the button all the way in with your thumb, hold it there, slide the piece on, and keep sliding until the button reaches the hole and pops out on its own. That pop is the click.

Plain-language comparison Atira approved for customers: "the same kind of spring button that holds a collapsable tent pole together." A cane or walker leg works too.

**Why:** This is the root cause behind a recurring "it won't click / I can't find the screws" ticket. Confirmed with Atira 2026-08-15 on ticket 287899154 (Elaine, Stability Shopper).

**How to apply:** When a customer says a part won't click or lock, check this before assuming a defect or missing hardware. Draft in the voice from [[gorgias-reply-tone]].
