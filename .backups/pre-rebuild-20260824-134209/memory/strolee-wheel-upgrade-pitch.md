When a customer complains about standard wheels struggling on outdoor surfaces, bumps, or rough terrain:

1. **Do not affirm the complaint or agree the product has a flaw.** Never say "huge difference" or validate claims about tipping/flipping.
2. **Do not offer upgraded wheels for free.**
3. **Acknowledge their experience without ignoring it.** Don't breeze past what they said or it reads as dismissive.
4. **Frame the standard wheels positively** as designed for smooth, everyday surfaces.
5. **Frame the upgraded wheels as an add-on for a different use case**, not a fix. Position them as being "for more active parents who are out on all kinds of terrain."

**Approved phrasing (from a real draft Atira approved 2026-08-16):**
"The standard wheels that come with the stroller are designed for smooth, everyday surfaces. For parents who are more active and out on all kinds of terrain, we created the upgraded wheel set as an add-on. They were designed specifically for surfaces like sidewalks, parks, and rougher outdoor areas so you get a smoother, more comfortable ride."

**Why:** This demographic can be triggered easily. Affirming wheel complaints invites warranty/refund demands. Ignoring their concerns reads as dismissive and makes it worse. The middle ground is acknowledging what they said, reframing the standard wheels as fit for purpose, and positioning the upgrade as a lifestyle choice.

**How to apply:** Any ticket where a customer complains about wheels on bumps, rough surfaces, sidewalks, etc. See [[strolee-never-affirm-defects]].
