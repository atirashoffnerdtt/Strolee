## Folding Steps (Stability Shopper / Pet Stroller)

From Gorgias macro "Folding- Shopper" (id 198123, 197 uses):
1. Slightly lift up on the handle
2. Squeeze the button on the handle (at the same time)
3. The entire cart will fold forward and clip in place when flat

Assembly video showing the fold: https://stroleecarts.com/pages/stability-shopper-how-to

This applies to both the Stability Shopper and the Pet Stroller & Personal Shopper 2-in-1 Bundle, since they share the same frame.

**Source:** Gorgias macro, confirmed 2026-08-17. Use these facts, not the macro's voice. See [[strolee-past-tone-rejected]].
