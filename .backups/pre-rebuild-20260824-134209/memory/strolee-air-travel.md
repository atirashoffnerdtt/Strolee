**The facts** (from Atira's own past replies across ~16 tickets): Strolee carts do not fit in an airline overhead bin. The customer can take the cart all the way through the airport and check it at the gate. Stability Shopper V2 folds to 23"W x 9"D x 25.5"H and weighs 13 lbs — the 25.5" height is what rules out the bin.

**Two hard limits on what we say:**
- **Never promise gate check is free.** Policies and fees vary by airline and Strolee is not airline customer service. Tell the customer to check with their airline.
- **Never imply airline damage is covered.** Once it's in the airline's hands, Strolee does not cover damage in transit. Say so plainly and kindly.

**Why:** Atira corrected a draft of mine on 2026-08-15 that said "check it at the gate free of charge" — Strolee can't guarantee another company's policy or warranty their handling.

**How to apply:** Answer flying questions with: won't fit overhead, gate check works, confirm policy and fees with the airline, damage in transit isn't covered. Note the existing "Overhead carry on bin single" macro (id 284330) claims strollers DO fit overhead and contradicts more recent replies — treat it as stale, don't reuse it.
