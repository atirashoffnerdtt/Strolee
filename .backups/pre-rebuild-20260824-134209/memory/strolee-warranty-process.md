For any warranty or replacement request, always ask the customer for a photo of the issue and their order number before proceeding.

**Why:** Atira specified this on 2026-08-16 as standard process for all warranty claims.

**How to apply:** Whenever a customer reports something broken or requests a replacement under warranty, ask for both a photo and the order number in the first reply.
