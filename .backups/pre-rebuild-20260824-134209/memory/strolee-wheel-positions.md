Front wheels are the swivelling casters, and they're what people mean by "catches," "sticks," or "stops on uneven ground." Stair climbing ($42) and beach ($42) wheels are **rear pairs, 2x each**. Only the all-terrain 4-pack replaces the fronts on a V2.

**Why:** Atira, 2026-08-22: *"for victoria shes saying the front wheels catch. the stair wheels go on the rear so offering them isn't helpful."* I offered stair wheels to a customer whose front wheels were the problem, which reads as not having listened to her.

She also caught a second error in the same draft: *"are you sure these have the swivel mount? I don't want you confusing her order for a personal shipping cart."* The **caster lock is a Personal Shopper feature** (there's a "Caster Locking" video on the personal-shopper-assembly page). Don't give that mechanism to a Stability Shopper owner. See [[strolee-personal-shopper-video-wrong-link]] for the same class of mistake.

**How to apply:** On steps and curbs the stair wheels pull the cart behind you. There is no video anywhere on the site showing wheels coming on or off. See [[strolee-accessory-compatibility]].
