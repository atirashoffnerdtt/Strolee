What actually makes a draft sound like AI, per Atira on 2026-08-16:

1. **Em dashes.** Use a comma or a period instead.
2. **Stacked hedging clauses**, specifically the "so it's always worth… so you know what to expect" shape — a recommendation padded with a second clause justifying it. Say it once and directly: "so I'd give yours a quick call before you fly."

**Just as important — what is NOT the problem.** I over-corrected once by also stripping "travels really well" and the friendly closing clauses, and Atira's response was "you removed all the niceness." Warm phrasing, light benefit clauses like "so it's easy to manage on travel days," and enthusiastic openers are the voice, not AI tells. Leave them alone.

**Why:** The fix for AI-sounding copy is surgical, not a rewrite. Flattening a warm draft into terse copy is a worse outcome than the original problem.

**How to apply:** When Atira says something sounds like AI, hunt for em dashes and doubled hedging clauses first, change only those, and hand her back her own wording everywhere else. Never rewrite a draft she has already warmed up. See [[gorgias-approved-reply-example]] and [[gorgias-draft-tone-corrections]].
