When a customer uses a casual or shorthand name for a product (e.g., "cart cover" for the Personal Shopper Rain Cover), use their term in the reply as long as it's clear what they mean. Don't correct them to the official product name.

**Why:** Atira corrected this on 2026-08-17. The reply should feel natural and meet the customer where they are, not sound like a product catalog.

**How to apply:** Mirror the customer's language for product names in the draft. Link to the correct product page so there's no confusion, but call it what they called it.
