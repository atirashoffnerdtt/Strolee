### Hard compatibility facts
- **Stair and beach wheels are V2 only** (black frame). They will not attach to a V1 silver frame.
- **A V1 owner cannot have stair wheels at all.** The clearance stair set is Original Shopper and
  Beach Cart only. There is no stair option for a V1, and the only route to one is a V2 cart. Say
  that plainly rather than implying a workaround exists.
- **Why, and it's a good answer:** we were working on making them fit the V1 frame and found it
  **would have compromised the frame's stability**, so we built them for the new frame instead
  rather than make that trade on a cart people lean on. Say "would have," not "did," so it stays
  about the design we didn't ship rather than the cart in their house.
- **Original Shopper owners: we DO still sell wheels for it.** The clearance listings, $22 and $28,
  in stock. They sit on `accessories-all` rather than the front of the site, which is why customers
  write in saying we only sell wheels for the beach and stability carts. Send the collection link.
- **The Personal Shopper has no upgraded or all-terrain set any more and there is no substitute.**
  Clearance is **leftover stock, not an upgrade**. Don't relabel it as "the sturdiest option that
  fits your cart."
- **V1 rear wheels need tools** and can't be swapped for any upgrade wheel. V2 rear wheels are
  tool-free.
- **Beach Cart wheels are Beach Cart only**, different mounts, not interchangeable with Shopper.

### Handling
- **Wheels come off with a silver tab, not a button.** A small **silver tab** where each wheel meets
  the frame. **Press and hold it and the wheel slides straight out.** No tools.
- **Rear axle swap:** push the small silver tabs both sides, slide the old out, slide the new in.
- **Front wheel "broken" or won't turn:** it's bound in the frame. Hold the silver tab and pull the
  wheel out to release it.
- **Front wheels lift or wobble when empty:** normal with no weight in the seat.
- **Brakes "don't work" with stair wheels on:** by design. Brakes work with standard, beach and
  upgraded wheels. A feature question, not a defect.
- **Stair wheels on steps and curbs: they pull the cart behind you**, gripping each step as it goes.
  They stay on for flat ground, no swapping back. V2 stair wheels work on all surfaces indoors and out.
- **The caster lock is a Personal Shopper feature.** Do **not** tell a Stability Shopper owner to
  check their swivel lock. Confirm with Shad before giving any front-wheel mechanism advice on a
  Stability Shopper.

## PATHS

### Wheel complaint (bumps, rough surfaces, tipping)
Do **not** affirm the complaint, agree there's a flaw, validate tipping claims, or offer upgraded
wheels free. Don't breeze past what they said either, that reads as dismissive. Approved phrasing:

> The standard wheels that come with the stroller are designed for smooth, everyday surfaces. For
> parents who are more active and out on all kinds of terrain, we created the upgraded wheel set as
> an add-on. They were designed specifically for surfaces like sidewalks, parks, and rougher outdoor
> areas so you get a smoother, more comfortable ride.

### "How do I take the wheels off"
The silver tab, pressed and held, and nothing else. **Do not attach the stair-wheel product link** —
that's a $42 product page and dropping it here reads as a pitch.

### V1 owner wants a V2-only wheel (or asks about "the larger wheels")
Say plainly it won't fit, say sorry about that, give the frame-stability reason, then
**ALWAYS include the V1 upgraded front wheels link** as the thing that DOES fit their frame:
`https://stroleecarts.com/products/upgraded-stability-shopper-front-wheels`. Then offer the
**$40 V2 upgrade** (they keep their current cart). See `discounts.md`.

**The V1 upgraded front wheels link is a must-send** on any V1 owner asking about larger, upgraded,
or better wheels. Not linking it sends them away with only a no.
