# Affiliates, influencers, press and wholesale

## GATE
- [ ] **Is this an approved Shopify Collabs affiliate, or an unsolicited pitch?** Two completely
      different replies. A Collabs approval notification opens with "Your application has been
      approved" and carries a link and code Shopify already generated.
- [ ] For a Collabs affiliate: **pull their actual link, code and commission rate out of the
      ticket.** They are sitting right there.
- [ ] For wholesale: which products, and roughly what quantity.

**GIVEAWAY: none.** Gifting is closed. Commission is not a giveaway.

## MUST SAY
- Collabs affiliate: their **actual** link, their **actual** code and what it gives their audience,
  and their **actual** commission rate. Never a generic "you have a unique affiliate link."
- Unsolicited pitch: we don't currently have an ambassador or partnership program, but it's
  something we hope to bring back.
- Wholesale: never decline a bulk request outright. Ask what and how many, then quote.

## FACTS

### Shopify Collabs approved affiliate
A real welcome, not a brush-off. **Affiliates can earn commission OR a discount**, both are on the
table, so don't describe it as commission-only.

Cover:
1. Welcome to the Strolee family
2. They're approved and set to start sharing
3. **Their actual affiliate link, their actual discount code and what it gives their audience, and
   their actual commission rate**, pulled from the ticket and never described generically
4. They can share on social, blogs, stories, wherever works best
5. **No required product** — highlight whichever Strolee stroller speaks to them and their audience

*(Atira may override the "no required product" line to point someone at a specific product, e.g. the
Double, if that's what the brand needs content for. Follow her direction when she does.)*

Closing: **Talk soon.**

### Unsolicited influencer / creator / UGC / ambassador pitch
A fresh inbound DM or comment forwarded into Gorgias, subjects like "Direct message with [handle],"
"Message from [name]," or a creator describing their following.

Standard answer: we don't currently have an ambassador or partnership program but it's something we
hope to bring back, and **we aren't offering gifted or paid collaborations, only commission on
sales.** Closing: **Take care.**

Application pages: `stroleecarts.com/pages/brand-ambassador` · `stroleebaby.com/pages/collab`.

**Gifting is closed.** *(Settled by Atira 2026-08-21.)* It was a real program through 2025, it isn't
now. Don't offer free product and don't hint it might be possible.

**Press and media** get the same handling as a collab pitch. No separate process exists.

**Most of this category is not spam.** The advertising tickets are mostly genuine comments on
Strolee's own ads and deserve a warm brief reply.

### Wholesale
**Wholesale is real** and case-by-case quoting, not a published program. Never decline a bulk
request outright, ask which products and roughly what quantity, then quote.
- **3-9 carts:** accessories 35% off, Stability/pet bundle 30% off (~$182/unit)
- **10+ carts:** another 5% off (accessories 40%, bundle 35%, ~$169/unit)
- Discounts start as low as **3 units**, cap around **50% off** at high volume
- Accessories are discounted harder than carts, higher margin

**Private label** (client logo on the cart): 30% deposit, **minimum 50 units**, 45-60 day lead time.
Cheaper workaround with no stated minimum: embroider the logo on the organizer accessory only and
sell it as a bundle.

### Distribution
**Otherwise closed.** Our own sites, Amazon, and TikTok. Nowhere else.

**TikTok Shop: we can't do anything on that side.** Collabs codes work only on our own sites. We
can't issue TikTok Shop codes, samples or collaboration links. Say so plainly rather than offering
to look into it.

## PATHS
- **Approved Collabs affiliate:** the welcome above, with their real details. Talk soon.
- **Unsolicited pitch:** the standard answer, warm, brief. Take care.
- **Bulk buyer:** ask what and how many, then quote from the table.
- **TikTok Shop relay ticket** (sender `...@chat-seller-us.tiktok.com`): recommend closing, never
  draft. See `00-router.md`.

## TRAPS
- **Never write a generic affiliate welcome.** The link, the code and the rate are in the ticket.
  Leaving them out creates extra back-and-forth for no reason.
- **Don't confuse an approved Collabs affiliate with a pitch.** One gets a welcome, one gets the
  no-program answer.
- **Don't offer gifted product.** Gifting is closed.
- **A real email is still a real email.** Someone writing from their own address about TikTok, a
  TikTok sample, or a collab is a normal ticket and gets a real reply.
