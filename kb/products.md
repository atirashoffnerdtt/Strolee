# Products — what exists, which generation, what's discontinued

## GATE
Pin the **generation** before answering anything. Not the name, the generation.
- [ ] Read the product and variant off the Shopify order on the ticket.
- [ ] Check the **order date**. Any Stability Shopper ordered before **July 1 2026** is a V1,
      whatever colour the variant says.
- [ ] If the order data doesn't settle it, ask the one identifying question rather than guessing.

**The fastest identifying question:** *is there a bar going across the rear wheels?*
**Bar = Personal Shopper. No bar = Stability Shopper.**

## MUST SAY
- Mirror the customer's own name for a product. Don't correct them to the catalog name.
- Before it ships: "we hope you **love** your Strolee." After they have it: "**loving**."

**Frame colour is not the variant colour, and the order date beats both.** V1 sold in Black, Blue
and Shadow Grey, so an order line reading "Stability Shopper - Black" from 2025 is still a **V1**.
"Black frame = V2" refers to the frame itself.

**"Mobility Strolee" is not a product.** A customer nickname for the Stability Shopper. Don't
correct them, just answer about the Stability Shopper.

**"v2" means different things on the two sites.** On `stroleebaby.com`, v2 = the Double / Baby+ v2
stroller. On `stroleecarts.com`, V2 = the Stability Shopper. Cart owners buy the Baby-site wheels by
mistake constantly.

### Carts

**Stability Shopper V2** — $195 · Special $165 (`/products/strolee-stability-shopper-special`) ·
regular `stroleecarts.com/products/strolee-stability-shopper`
- Open 27"D x 23"W x 38.5"H · Folded 23"W x 9"D x 25.5"H · 13 lbs · handle 38.5" from ground
- Upper basket 55 lbs · lower basket 20 lbs · frame stability 300 lbs · pet bassinet 50 lbs
- **Included:** frame, personal shopping bag (21"L x 12.5"W x 15"D), cup holder
- **Not included:** everything in the accessory table. Product photos show the mesh caddy attached,
  which misleads.
- 2-year warranty · 30-day risk-free trial · **ships fully assembled**, customer pops the wheels on
- **The handle is fixed at 38.5" and does not adjust.** Standing answer: *"the handle on the
  stability shopper is fixed at the 38.5" height. There is no rear axle on the cart though so you
  are able to adjust how close you walk to it."* An adjustable handle is something we hope to add,
  no date. *(Confirmed 2026-08-24.)*

**Colors:** Black · Blue ("Main Blue") · Shadow · Sage · Berry. Sage is a soft light green, site
photos are true to it. *Checked 2026-08-22: Berry sold out on both pages; Shadow sold out on the
Special page, available on the regular page.* Live stock: `stroleecarts.com/products/<handle>.js`

**The "Special" is just the regular V2 at a limited-time price.** Same product, same inclusions.
$195 → $165. See `discounts.md` for how the $30 comes off and why it can't stack.

**Pet Bassinet v3** — $75. Folds flat. Two metal bars clip into the bottom to hold it open; unclip
and it lays down. Fold shown at the **1:30 mark** of `stroleecarts.com/pages/pet-stroller-assembly`.
Drops straight in once the shopping bag is lifted out. Fits every version of the Stability Shopper.
Holds 50 lbs, sits ~33" off the ground, level with the basket. Canopy folds down but doesn't come
off. Two leashes built in. A sleeve on the side lets a seatbelt thread through to secure it in a car.
**The outer shell does not come off the frame.** Clean it in place: lay it flat first by unclipping
the two bars, then spot clean or hand wash, and let it dry fully before storing.
**Safe for car use with the lap belt.** The bassinet page has a "Safe Place for Them to Ride"
section: *"Drive confidently, knowing your fur baby is safely secured yet still enjoying the scenic
views. Simply use your cars lap belt to help secure the bassinet in place."* The strap on the side
**opens with velcro and wraps around the lap portion of the seatbelt** — you don't thread anything
through it. Open the velcro, lay it over the lap belt, press it back down, buckle in as normal.
Customers who describe it as their pet's car seat get a warm validation, not a correction.
*(Corrected 2026-08-25 against the live product page and Atira's clarification.)*

**Stability Pet V2** — `/products/strolee-stability-pet`. Ships with both the pet bassinet and the
personal shopper bag, converts between either config anytime. Carts always ship with the latest
bassinet.

**Beach Cart** — $179. Holds up to 100 lbs. Street-wheel and beach-wheel configurations.
**Explicitly not stability-rated**, it can't support weight on the handle the way the Stability Cart
can, so never describe it that way. Replacement bags $15, newest style only, **not listed anywhere
on the site**, so they go out by invoice. **Its lower storage compartment fits the beach cart only**
and is not the top tray. *(Live catalog check 2026-08-24.)*

**Original Shopper** (Personal Shopper / Personal Shopping Cart) — discontinued, replaced by the
Stability Shopper. **Completely different model.** New accessories, wheels and upgrades do not fit.

### Strollers

**Destination vs Linq** — both fit overhead bins.
- Destination: more compact, upgraded-wheel option, handle 40" from floor, 12.5 lbs, folded
  8.5"D x 17.5"W x 22"H
- Linq: holds a car seat, handle 42", no rear axle (better for taller parents with longer strides)

**Double vs Double Lite** — Lite is a limited-edition lower-cost version of the V2 Double.
- Lite does **not** include: backpack, rain cover, snack trays (sold separately)
- Lite does **not** have: adjustable handlebar, deeper seats
- Harness: Lite = 5-point magnetic buckle · V2 = 3-point no-rethread
- Parent pockets: Lite = velcro · V2 = zippers
- Weight capacity: Lite 50 lbs/seat · V2 60 lbs/seat
- Stroller weight: V2 Double 22 lbs · Lite 19.7 lbs
- Both tested down to **3 months**. Folds ~90% of the way with car seat adapters attached.
- Identical on both: fabrics, wheels, belly bar, seat cushion, canopy size. Accessories interchange.

**Single belly bar** (customers say "lap bar") — $17.90,
`stroleebaby.com/products/single-belly-bar-for-strolee-double`. Fits all single Strolee strollers.
The Double includes a 2-section bar; the single bar frees one side for a car seat adapter.

**Linq bars (stroller connectors) — discontinued, will not be restocked.** The Linq system page
(`stroleebaby.com/pages/strolee-linq`) is still live and still describes connecting two strollers,
which keeps generating these tickets. Approved wording:

> The Linq bars are no longer available, and we won't be restocking them, I'm sorry about that! Our
> double stroller is more popular. Our Linq strollers are still available on their own, and they can
> be pushed side by side.

### Replacement parts we do NOT sell
*Checked 2026-08-22 on stroleebaby.com.* There is **no replacement seat cover, seat fabric, seat
cushion, canopy, or seat liner** for the Double. The only Double accessories are the Parent Organizer
($55), Rain Cover ($49.90), Snack Tray ($19.90), Snack Cups, and Carrying Straps (from $9.90). If
fabric damage isn't covered under warranty there is nothing to link them to. Don't offer one.

## PATHS
- **Discontinued and they want it:** say plainly it's no longer available and won't be restocked,
  add "I'm sorry about that!", then give them somewhere to go. Never a bare no.
- **They have a legacy model:** check the Legacy Register before saying a part is available. If the
  model is gone and the warranty is live, see `warranty.md` for the discount ladder.
- **Not launched yet:** see `discounts.md` → Restocks. No dates, ever.

## TRAPS
- **Don't correct their product name.** They said "cart cover," you say "cart cover," and link the
  right page.
- **Don't assume the variant colour tells you the generation.** Check the order date.
- **The Original Shopper is not an older Stability Shopper.** It is a different cart. Nothing new
  fits it.
- **Never describe the Beach Cart as stability-rated.**
- **It's a velcro wrap, not a sleeve to thread.** Someone asking "how do I secure it in the car"
  is asking about install, not safety rating. Answer the install: open the velcro, lay the lap belt
  in, close it back up.
