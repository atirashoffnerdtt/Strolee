# Known fixes — try these before authorizing anything

Every one of these closes a ticket with nothing shipped.

## GATE
- [ ] **Which product and which generation?** The fix differs. See `products.md`.
- [ ] Have they already tried it? Read the whole thread first.
- [ ] For a Stability Shopper, send `stability-shopper-how-to`. **Never** `personal-shopper-assembly`.

**GIVEAWAY: none.** Troubleshooting ships nothing. Don't promise the part before they've tried the fix.

## MUST SAY
- **Never tell someone nothing is wrong with it.** They've usually already tried. Give the
  instruction, then leave the door open for it genuinely being faulty.
- Snap buttons pop **OUT**, never "up." They're on the underside.
- Plain everyday comparisons over technical part names.

## FACTS

### Snap buttons / "I'm missing screws"
There are **no screws anywhere**. Small spring-loaded metal buttons on the **UNDERSIDE** lock frame
parts together. **Always tell them to look underneath** — this is the single most important line in
any of these replies.

- Sequence: press the button all the way in with your thumb, **hold it**, slide the piece on, keep
  sliding until the button reaches the hole and **pops out on its own**. That pop is the click.
  **Never tell them to line the button up with the hole themselves.**
- Say the button pops **OUT**, never "up."
- **Stability Shopper cross bar (manual step 3):** the buttons are on the underside of the bars **on
  the cart**, not on the cross bar. The two halves of the cross bar slide together and don't click
  into each other; the click comes from the cart's buttons.
- **Common cause:** the button **slipped down inside the tube** during shipping, so there's nothing
  to press. Drop a pen or small screwdriver into the open end and push gently, it pops back out.
- Approved comparison: *"the same kind of spring button that holds a collapsable tent pole
  together."* A cane or walker leg works too.

Snap buttons also hold the **Double v2 rear axle** on, and the wheels release a different way.
For anything wheel-related see `wheels.md`.

### Stroller won't stand up
One of the highest-volume tickets. The kickstands ship stowed so they don't get damaged and need
releasing. Push the small bar in, rotate 180°, it drops into place. The Double has one per side
("kickstand on **each** side"), singles have one ("kickstand on the side"). Re-stow before gate
checking.

**Canonical wording, from macro 270337** (used dozens of times, most recently 288893220):
> There is a kickstand on [each side / the side] of the stroller that is stowed during shipping
> which needs to be opened. Just push the small bar in, rotate it 180° & then it will be self
> standing. For reference, it's best to stow this again if you gate check on the airplane so it
> doesn't get damaged with the luggage.

### Cart won't stand
The two small bars below the handle slid in during shipping. Pull them out further.

### Cart won't open
Lift the handle **all the way**. Not lifting fully is the common failure.

### Cart won't fold
Lift the handle slightly *and* squeeze the handle button at once, then push **forward and slightly
down**, not backward. On carts with a green arrow button: green arrow unlocks it to roll, the handle
button folds it.

Folding on the Stability Shopper and Pet Stroller (same frame): lift the handle slightly, squeeze
the handle button at the same time, the whole cart folds forward and clips in place when flat.
**Send BOTH links, every time:**
1. The how-to page: `https://stroleecarts.com/pages/stability-shopper-how-to`
2. The founder walkthrough on the product listing, under the add to cart button:
   `https://stroleecarts.com/products/strolee-stability-shopper` — Atira's line for this second
   link: *"We also have a great video on the listing of our founder walking you through another way.
   It is under the add to cart button!"*

**Both, not one.** The customer often can't tell one is even there without the second pointer.
*(Gorgias macro "Folding- Shopper", id 198123, 197 uses.)*

### Won't clip open — cart (Stability Shopper)
1. Fully release the button on the handle
2. Push the handle down and back slightly, a quick swift "snap" motion helps
3. Try it with the shopping bag removed so the latching mechanism is visible
4. The small piece needs to go inside the larger piece to clip open

*(Reference photo in Gorgias macro 220545.)*

### Won't clip open — single / Baby+
1. Fully release the button on the handle
2. Push the handle down and back, quick swift "snap"
3. While it extends, don't just let the front wheels smack the ground
4. If it's open but not clipped: thumb on top of the handle, pointer finger behind it where it goes
   down toward the seat, clip it open that way

### Won't clip open — Double
A quick swift **"snap upward"** motion when opening ensures the stroller clips open. Place
**thumbs in front of the handle with pointer fingers behind**, and snap it open. Short video that
shows this: `https://cdn.shopify.com/videos/c/o/v/dacbb81c36c643bd921ff3bf9bace420.mov`

*(Gorgias macro "Double clipping open", id 285887.)*

### Stroller won't open
Quick upward snap, thumbs in front of the handle, index fingers behind. The latch is the small
circle directly under the handle.

### "It collapses / it won't lock"
**If red is showing at the side locking mechanism it is not locked. No red = locked.** Test by
flicking the handle or pushing the basket down while holding the handle. Strollers show a red bar on
the side when open and locked.

### Nuna car seat won't seat
Set it onto the adapters **from behind**, lined up, then squeeze the foot area slightly and it
slides in.

### Wheels, axles, brakes, front wheels
See `wheels.md`.

### Air travel
Carts do **not** fit in an overhead bin (V2 Shopper folds to 25.5" tall, that's what rules it out).
They can take it through the airport and gate check it. Strollers, Destination and Linq, **do** fit
overhead bins.
- **Never promise gate check is free.** Fees and policies vary by airline. Tell them to check with
  their airline.
- **Never imply airline damage is covered.** Once it's in the airline's hands Strolee doesn't cover
  transit damage. Say so plainly and kindly.

## PATHS
- **All latch tickets:** troubleshoot before offering a return. If troubleshooting doesn't resolve
  it, ask for a photo of the latching mechanism.
- **Still broken after the fix:** now it's a warranty claim. See `warranty.md` for the gate.

## TRAPS
- **Never affirm the product is unsafe or defective.**
- **Never send `personal-shopper-assembly` to a Stability Shopper owner.** That's a discontinued
  product and it tells someone with a current cart to follow instructions for a cart that isn't
  theirs.
- **Don't promise the part before they've tried the fix.** "If that doesn't work I'll send you a new
  one" pre-commits the shipment and most people will simply take it.
- **Soften the problem description.** "Slipped down inside" not "fell inside."
- **Gorgias macro 284330** ("Overhead carry on bin single") claims carts fit overhead. Stale, don't
  reuse it.
