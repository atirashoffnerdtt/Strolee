# Delivery problems, missing items, wrong items, damage and delays

## GATE
- [ ] **Read the carrier and status off the order**, never from memory. The archive has a customer
      told two different carriers on consecutive days.
- [ ] **What does tracking actually show?** Delivered, stalled, never scanned, or returning to
      sender. Three different situations with three different answers.
- [ ] **Confirmed shipping address, asked for in this reply**, before any reship. Always, even when
      the order shows one and even when the address was never the problem.
- [ ] Damage or wrong item: **photos or a short video first.** Mandatory, no exceptions.

**GIVEAWAY: yes** on any reship, replacement part, or refund. **None** when they open the carrier
case themselves.

## MUST SAY
- Name the carrier the order shows.
- On a carrier case: give them the tracking number and the carrier's contact
  (FedEx 1-800-463-3339 / fedex.com).
- Never concede when they say the carrier blamed us.

## FACTS

### Shipping and carriers
**Where they're made:** *"These are manufactured in our partner facility in Asia & shipped from our
warehouse in Charlotte, NC."* That exact wording is the standing answer.
*(Confirmed 2026-08-24.)*

Ships from the **Charlotte, North Carolina warehouse**. Warehouse only, no storefront pickup.
**US only, no Canada.** Carts ship to **residential addresses only**.
Overseas customers have two routes: the EU storefront `stroleecarts.eu`, or **shipping to a US
address themselves and forwarding it on**. Take the second if they suggest it, it's a normal US order.

| | |
|---|---|
| Carts and strollers | **FedEx** (default) |
| Some Strolee Baby orders | **UPS**, also often used for reshipments |
| Small accessories and parts | **USPS**, can be requested for a replacement part |
| Hawaii and Alaska | **FedEx SmartPost** (FedEx hands to USPS for the last mile). Biggest source of chronic delays. Large surcharge. |

- **Ships 1-2 business days**, **3-5 business days** in transit. "Most orders arrive within about a
  week."
- **Shipping cost:** $8.95 on carts. Strolee Baby standard ~$6.95. **No published free-shipping
  threshold** on the website (checked 2026-08-27). Don't tell a customer "free over $X" — we can't
  point at where that threshold is stated.
- Boxes often arrive **opened and retaped**. That's us adding purchased accessories, not damage.
  Say so plainly.
- **Accessories ship inside the stroller or cart box** — adapters, wheels, seat liners, organizers.
  Check there before treating anything as missing.

## PATHS

### Delivery problems — three cases, three different answers

**Verify before you remedy.** A large share resolve as "found." First reply confirms the shipping
address on the order, sends the carrier's delivery photo, and asks whether there's a building office
or front desk and who signed.

| What tracking shows | What we do |
|---|---|
| **Delivered to the address they entered** | **No reship, no refund.** They open the case themselves with their tracking number. Closing: **Take care.** |
| **Stalled, lost, or never scanned** | **We reship free**, or refund in full if they'd rather wait no longer. Say which it is and let them pick, in one message. |
| **Carrier never attempted, returning to sender** | **Ours to fix.** Reship free. Switching carrier on the reship is reasonable if they ask. Don't make them open a case for a package coming back to us anyway. |
| **Package came back to us** | **Returned / undeliverable / no reason given → reship it** (284138955, 258353800; verify the address first if anything looks off, 269468908). **Customer REFUSED delivery → refund it** (256766698), and they're welcome to reorder. |

**Stalled tracking is not "delivered but not received."** If the last scan is days or weeks old and
it was never marked delivered, that's a lost shipment and we reship free.

**Porch theft: we do nothing.** Confirmed delivery to the address they entered, then taken, is not
ours. No reship, no refund, no discount. Warm, direct, no door left open.

**Blocks on any reship:** an open chargeback, or an order already refunded.

**UPS is the same as FedEx.** Customer opens the delivery claim directly with UPS using their
tracking number. UPS investigates where the package was left and works toward resolution.

**Wrong address the customer entered:** their mistake, their cost, no free reship, especially on
high-ticket items. The address on the order at checkout is what shipped. Two options: return label
plus a new order, or return and reship at their expense (~$35-$55 plus reshipping). Tell them to
price a carrier-direct forward first and only use us if it's cheaper. Be warm but direct, clarify
the facts, and **don't leave the door open** for a resolution from our side. Closing: **Take care.**

### Missing item from a shipment

**Our problem, not the carrier's — the opposite of a lost package.**

**We are usually not missing things.** Treat it as "it hasn't been found yet," not "we failed to
pack it." Do not open by conceding, do not lead with a free replacement.

**Check-first covers every item.** Wheels, adapters, bags, organizers, all of it. The cart ships
fully assembled with the wheels packed loose, so a wheel in the box padding is exactly what gets
missed. The parent cup holder settles into the lower basket and blends in, the number one
missing-item ticket, and most resolve as found.

**Use this wording.** From macro **262668**, 223 uses, picked by Atira over a draft of mine because
it *"sounded more pleasant."* Match this register for any item:

> Thanks so much for reaching out and I would be happy to assist! Can you please just confirm it is
> not in the lower basket for me? Many times in shipping the cup holder ends in the lower basket &
> blends right in so people don't notice it at first :)- I look forward to hearing back getting this
> resolved for you.

What makes it land: it **asks a favor** rather than issuing an instruction, and it **normalizes by
blaming shipping and the object**, not the person. It doesn't front-load the remedy.

If it's genuinely missing: **reship the piece free. No return, no photo, no waiting period.**
Common false positive: the item was never on the order. Quote the order lines and offer an invoice.

Closing: **Talk soon.**

### Wrong item shipped
**Ask for photos of what they received before offering any remedy.**
- **Our error, low-value:** ship the right one immediately, they keep or dispose of the wrong one.
- **Our error, high-value:** prepaid return label emailed and the replacement arranged. We pay
  return shipping.
- **They ordered the wrong thing:** normal return terms, refund reduced by actual shipping both
  ways. See `returns.md`.

### Damaged in transit
**Photos or a short video are mandatory before any remedy.** No exceptions, both shops, every agent.

Ladder, in the order actually used:
1. **Self-fix if it just came apart in shipping.** Small screwdriver down the tube to pop the silver
   snap piece out, then slide together until it clicks. Resolves a lot of them. See
   `troubleshooting.md`.
2. **Replacement part, free** — belly bar, snack tray, footrest, wheel, cup holder, bassinet, liner
3. **Full replacement unit.** Strollers require a photo of the **safety harness straps cut off**
   first. Hard safety gate, never waived. Carts generally need nothing back.
4. **Partial refund instead of a replacement** — archive shows $20, $25, $50, $75
5. **Full refund or return**

- **Damaged box, undamaged product = no remedy.** Ask them to open it and confirm first.
- **Past 30 days it's warranty only** (replacement part), not a return or refund.
- **Free and gift items aren't replaced** if the product still works safely.

### Delays
Actionable the moment tracking shows no movement or a stalled scan.
- **Expedited paid but not met:** refund the **difference** between what they paid and standard, not
  the whole amount.
- A delay that will miss their date is grounds for cancel and full refund pre-delivery, and a late
  arrival has been accepted as a return reason.

## TRAPS
- **Never apologize for slow service**, even when they had to chase us. Thank them for following up,
  then state what was done.
- **Don't concede a missing item.** Ask them to check, as a favor, and wait for the answer before
  offering anything.
- **Don't front-load the remedy.** Offering the replacement in the same breath as the ask makes the
  ask sound like a formality.
- **"A lot of people miss it" is banned.** Blame the shipping or the object, never the person.
- **Don't tell a customer to refuse delivery.** See `orders.md`.
- **Boxes arriving opened and retaped is us**, not damage.
