# Warranty

## GATE — all of these before anything ships
- [ ] **Photo or short video of the problem.** Carts: video *from the side* so the locking mechanism
      is visible. Folding complaints: video of the fold attempt.
- [ ] **Order number. Hard requirement.** A name, an old email, or "I bought it last July" is **not**
      the order number. Those are leads for a lookup, and the lookup has to actually happen and come
      back with a real order before anything ships.
- [ ] **Confirmed current shipping address**, asked for in this reply. These often changed since
      purchase.
- [ ] **Whole-unit stroller replacements only: photo of the seat belt straps cut off.** Mandatory,
      no exceptions, ever.

Anything missing means the reply **collects** and promises nothing. Never "here's your replacement,
also what's your order number."

**GIVEAWAY: yes.** Any free part or free unit gets flagged to Atira above the draft.

## MUST SAY
- Warranty covers **manufacturing defects only**.
- **2 years from purchase.** The $24.99 extension takes it to **5 years total**, not 2+5.
- It **does not transfer** to a secondhand owner.
- When declining: say sorry for the news, and give a paid path forward. Never a bare no.

## FACTS

**2 years from purchase**, standard on every product. **$24.99 buys an extension to 5 years total**
(not 2+5), can be added after shipping by emailed invoice and removed with a refund on request.

- **No registration exists.** Tracked digitally against name, address or order number. Tell
  customers there's no paperwork to keep, they ask constantly.
- **It does not transfer.** Secondhand owners aren't covered. Say so, offer the paid path.
- **Amazon orders are honored** with an Amazon order ID.

**Covers manufacturing defects only, and that applies to every part equally** — belly bars, wheels,
buckles, frames, all of it. There is no list of covered and uncovered parts. The archive looks
contradictory (a belly bar sent free one week, sold for $17.90 the next) only because the real
question was never the part.

**The real question is always: does the evidence show a manufacturer defect?** A judgment call on
the photo or video. The test used in past replies is **arrival condition**: "if it had come like
that we would have replaced it." Something that broke in normal use after months is not the same as
something wrong out of the box.

**Not covered:** customer-caused damage, pet damage, mold from storage, normal wear, secondhand and
resold units. Decline with a paid path forward, never a bare no.

**What ships free:** any defective part, or the whole unit. **No dollar threshold** — full strollers
and carts at $195-$369+ ship free under warranty. Next business day, 2-4 days transit.

**Customer buys:** belly bars ($17.90), upgraded wheels, accessory bags and organizers, add-ons.

### Most common failures — what actually breaks
Ranked by replacement parts arranged, Jun 2025 to Aug 2026:

| Rank | Part | Tickets |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Harness buckle / clasp** | ~100 |
| 2 | Rear axle | ~45 |
| 3 | Wheels (front most often) | ~40 |
| 4 | Whole stroller or cart | ~35 shipped free |
| 5 | Snap buttons | ~20 (plus many resolved by the pen fix) |
| 6 | Belly bar | ~18 (mostly sold, not replaced) |
| 7 | Frame | ~10 |

The harness buckle is number one by a wide margin. **We CAN replace the buckle as a part** — treat
these as a small-part warranty ship (photo + order number + address), not a whole-unit swap. Whole
units go out only when something bigger is actually broken.

## PATHS

**Troubleshoot first.** Most of these close with nothing shipped. See `troubleshooting.md` before
authorizing anything, and don't promise the part before they've tried the fix.

**Order genuinely can't be found and the part is small:** offer the $3 shipping invoice. Atira's
wording: *"Or since it's such a small piece we can send you an invoice for $3 just to cover shipping
and since we can't tie it to an order in our system."*

**Secondhand or bought locally:** warranty does not transfer. Say so, offer the paid path.

**Model is discontinued and no parts remain:** no free replacement. Offer a discount on the current
model, they keep the old unit. Archive range: **$40 off carts, $50-$60 off Baby+/Linq/beach,
$100 off Double Lite, $150 off Double V2.** Where the warranty is live but the exact model is gone,
send the current-generation equivalent free instead.

**Whole stroller replacement:** nothing ships until the cut-harness photo is in hand. The old unit
is not returned, they dispose of it.

**Defect replacements require nothing back.** They are not returns. See `returns.md`.

## TRAPS
- **Never affirm the defect.** Reassure by acting, not by conceding. See `CLAUDE.md` hard rule 1.
- **Never say "that's everything I needed"** when the order has not actually been found.
- **Don't skip troubleshooting** on something that reads like a known fix.
- **Don't assume a defect.** Age matters. Months of normal use is not out-of-the-box.
- **Don't give front-wheel mechanism advice on a Stability Shopper.** The caster lock is a Personal
  Shopper feature. See `wheels.md`.
- **The warranty page says "no fine print"** and lists no exclusions, while pet damage, mold, wear
  and secondhand are all declined. Unsettled, see `99-open.md`.
