# Accessories and car seat adapters

## GATE
- [ ] Which cart or stroller do they own, and which generation. See `products.md`.
- [ ] For a car seat adapter: **which car seat do they have**, by exact model. Not the brand, not
      the travel system name.
- [ ] Check the live product page before confirming stock. Several of these sell out.

## MUST SAY
- All accessories can be bought on their own, no cart purchase required. Send the collection link
  when someone asks how to order separately.
- Link everything they can actually buy. "How do I order" is answered with URLs.
- **Don't mention a V2-only accessory to a V1 customer.** They can't use it and it makes a ticket.

### Car seat adapters, stroller line — `stroleebaby.com/products/strolee-double-car-seat-adapter`

Two variants, both compare-at $55: **Nuna / Maxi-Cosi / Cybex / Clek / Britax $39** ·
**Chicco / Graco $44**. The difference is **$5**, invoiced if moving up, refunded if moving down.

Page lists compatible strollers as "Strolee Double V2, LINQ V2, Baby+".
Compatible seats: Nuna Pipa / Pipa Lite RX / Pipa Aire RX / Pipa URBN / Klik · Maxi-Cosi Mico Luxe &
Mico Max · Cybex Cloud Q & Aton Q · Clek · Britax Willow · Chicco KeyFit 30 & 35 · Graco SnugRide
Lite 35 LX & LX Infant. **Evenflo and Joie not supported yet.**

The adapter is necessary for safety, the only tested way to keep the seat in place.

- **"Chicco Bravo" is not a car seat**, it's a travel system and the bundled seat varies:
  Bravo Trio = KeyFit 30 (on our list) · Bravo Sport = KeyFit Sport (not) · Bravo LE Trio = KeyFit
  Max ClearTex (not) · Bravo Primo Trio = KeyFit Max Zip ClearTex (not). **When a customer says
  "Bravo" or just "KeyFit," ask which KeyFit model before confirming compatibility.**
- Baby+ and Double hold car seats. The **stroller** adapters do not fit the Stability Shopper, but
  **the cart has its own separate adapter** — see the table above.
- **Website discrepancy (confirmed 2026-08-22):** the adapter page lists "Baby+" as compatible and
  that link resolves to the **Baby+ v2** page because the original Baby+ listing is a draft. Say
  plainly that the link points at v2; don't guess at the fit.
- **Nuna install catches people:** set the seat onto the adapters **from behind**, line it up, then
  squeeze the foot area slightly and it slides in.
- Baby+ v2 basket side converts to hold a car seat or the pet bassinet. On the ORIGINAL Baby+, Shad
  told a customer in Sept 2025 the basket side was only interchangeable with the pet bassinet.

### What's included with a cart
See `products.md`. Short version: frame, personal shopping bag, cup holder. **The mesh caddy is
not included** — the main product images show it attached, which is why people think it is.

## PATHS
- **"How do I order accessories on their own?"** Send
  `stroleecarts.com/collections/stability-shopper-accessories`. No cart purchase needed.
- **Wrong car seat adapter ordered:** ask **which car seat they have** first, not which adapter they
  want. They may have the right one and be installing it wrong. See `returns.md` → Exchanges.
- **Sold out:** no date, point at the email list. See `discounts.md` → Restocks.

## TRAPS
- **Don't assume "KeyFit" is a KeyFit 30.** Ask which model.
- **The Deluxe Organizer clips on. There is no velcro on the cart.**
- **The mesh caddy is not included with the cart**, however the photos look.
- **Don't offer a Double replacement seat cover, canopy or liner.** They don't exist.
